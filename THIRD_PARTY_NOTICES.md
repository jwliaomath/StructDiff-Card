## US-align

StructDiff Card invokes the US-align executable and the Docker image compiles the
vendored official single-file source distribution. Its SHA-256 is checked in
`Dockerfile`; provenance is recorded in `vendor/USalign/README.md`.

## Example coordinates

The precomputed public demonstration uses PDB entries 2HHB and 1HHO downloaded
from the RCSB Protein Data Bank. Coordinate files remain attributed to their
depositors and the wwPDB/RCSB PDB data policies apply.
